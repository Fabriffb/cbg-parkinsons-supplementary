Project overview

This study integrates network pharmacology, density functional theory (DFT), and molecular docking to investigate candidate targets through which CBG may be relevant to Parkinson’s disease (PD).

The selected proteins include ESR1, EGFR, HSP90AA1, and PPARG. Docking scores were compared across AutoDock, AutoDock Vina, and DOCK6, and predicted interactions between CBG and ESR1 were considered alongside those of estradiol (E2).
